Alzheimer's Disease Data Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis | R, RStudio, ggplot2

Project Overview

This project explores the relationships between demographic characteristics, lifestyle behaviors, clinical measurements, and Alzheimer's disease diagnoses through exploratory data analysis (EDA).

The goal is to identify patterns and potential risk factors associated with Alzheimer's disease while demonstrating practical skills in data cleaning, statistical analysis, and data visualization using R.

Dataset

The analysis examines a dataset containing 2,149 patient records and 35 variables, including:

Demographics: Age, gender, ethnicity, and education level

Lifestyle Factors: BMI, smoking, alcohol consumption, physical activity, diet quality, and sleep quality

Medical History: Diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular disease, and family history

Clinical Measurements: Blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and cognitive assessments

Target Variable: Alzheimer's disease diagnosis

Tools & Technologies

R: Data cleaning, manipulation, and statistical analysis

RStudio: Development environment

ggplot2: Data visualization

R Markdown: Reproducible analysis and reporting

Methodology

1. Data Cleaning and Preparation

Reviewed dataset structure, variable types, and missing values.

Removed non-informative identifiers and prepared categorical variables for analysis.

Organized the dataset for exploratory analysis.

2. Exploratory Data Analysis

Calculated descriptive statistics to understand patient characteristics.

Created visualizations to examine distributions and differences between diagnosis groups.

Explored relationships between demographic, lifestyle, and clinical variables.

Used correlation analysis to investigate associations among numerical variables.

3. Interpretation

Compared patient characteristics across Alzheimer's diagnosis groups.

Examined cognitive assessment scores and potential relationships with diagnosis.

Identified patterns to inform further statistical investigation.

Key Findings

The dataset included 760 patients diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease.

Cognitive assessment scores, including MMSE, were examined across diagnosis groups.

Demographic, lifestyle, and clinical characteristics were explored to identify potential associations with Alzheimer's disease.

These findings represent exploratory associations rather than evidence of causation.
